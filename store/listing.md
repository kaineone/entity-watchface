## Description

Entity is online.

The time fills the screen in big block numerals, hour over minute. Along the bottom is a row of
bars that shows whether your watch can still reach your phone. Flick your wrist and a red bar
glides side to side across them, then settles. When the connection drops, the bars go flat and
grey, and the watch gives one short buzz so you know.

The top line shows the date and the temperature. Double tap the watch to see today's steps and
your heart rate for ten seconds.

On round watches the bars become tick marks around the edge of the screen. The red one steps round
once a second, a full lap each minute, then turns at the top and comes back. A double tap there
shows your steps.

It looks after your battery. The animation stops on its own when the battery runs low, during
quiet time, and while a timeline peek covers the screen. You can also turn it off.

In the settings you can choose 12 or 24 hour time, a red, cream or gold hour, Celsius or
Fahrenheit, whether to show the weather, the battery level where the animation stops, and whether
the watch buzzes when your phone disconnects. You can also turn off the double tap.

Works on the Pebble Time 2, Pebble Round 2, Pebble Time, Time Steel, Time Round and Pebble 2 Duo.
The temperature comes from Open-Meteo, using your phone's approximate location. Heart rate needs a
watch with a heart-rate sensor.
