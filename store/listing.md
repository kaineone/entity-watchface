## Description

Entity is online.

Big numbers for the hour and the minute, and along the bottom a row of bars that shows whether
your watch can still reach your phone. While it can, a red bar scans back and forth. When the
connection drops, the bars go flat and grey, and the watch gives one short buzz so you know.

The top line shows the date and the weather. Tap the watch to swap it for today's steps and your
heart rate for a few seconds. On round watches the bars run around the edge of the screen, and a
tap shows your steps.

It looks after your battery. The animation pauses on its own when the battery runs low or quiet
time starts, and while something covers the screen. You can also turn it off.

In the settings you can pick 12 or 24 hour time, a red, cream or gold hour, Celsius or Fahrenheit,
the battery level where the animation stops, and whether it buzzes when your phone disconnects.

Works on the Pebble Time 2, Pebble Round 2, Pebble Time, Time Steel, Time Round and Pebble 2 Duo.
Weather comes from Open-Meteo, using your phone's approximate location. Heart rate needs a watch
with a heart-rate sensor.
